# The 2019 open cube: assembly and the five gates

Built on VU HPC, 29 October 2026, by `scripts/build_cube.sbatch 2019`
(`experiments/open_cube/build_cube.py`). Figures are from **job 247673** (`main`,
4 CPU, 2 min 41 s), the run with the corrected S1 plausible range. Where the first run
(**job 247671**, wrong S1 range) differs, both are shown. Refusal causes are from
**job 247675** (`inspect_refusals.py`). Input: the 2019 fetch (job 247545,
`docs/hpc_setup.md`).

**Not yet decided: grid or point geometry.** Both are reported side by side below, with
no recommendation. The ablation has not been run.

## What the cube is

One sample per Sentinel-1 pass per station, 17 Twente stations, 2019:

| Element | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Target time | the S1 pass | — |
| Radar | S1 RTC γ⁰ VV, VH at the station pixel | linear power; S1 nodata at the station drops the sample |
| Optical | S2 L2A B02 B03 B04 B08 B11 B12 at the station pixel | the newest S2 **at or before** the pass that is usable at the station pixel, within 30 days |
| Ground truth | DANS Twente 5 cm calibrated SM | mean of flag-0 readings within ±30 min (CET → UTC); frozen soil dropped |

**Per-pixel masking at the station** (PLAN.md, matching rules 6–7). An S2 observation is
usable for a station only if all of these hold:
- the fetch did not tag it `station_measured: false`;
- SCL at the station pixel is 4 or 5;
- B02 ≥ 50 DN (this floor is questioned below).

SCL 6/7 at the station are excluded and counted. Cloud (8–10), shadow (3), snow (11) and
dark area (2) make that date unusable at the station, and the next older date is tried.

### Geometry: both modes, undecided

**S1/S2 CRS pairs:**

| S1 \| S2 | Samples | Grid mode | Point mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| EPSG:32632 \| 32632 | 2,474 | PASS | PASS_WITH_DECISION |
| EPSG:32631 \| 32632 | 843 | **REFUSE** | PASS_WITH_DECISION |
| EPSG:32632 \| 32631 | 136 | **REFUSE** | PASS_WITH_DECISION |
| EPSG:32631 \| 32631 | 52 | PASS | PASS_WITH_DECISION |

- **Grid mode refuses exactly the 979 mixed-zone samples (27.9%)**, all for "layers
  declare 2 distinct CRS". Every same-zone sample passes, including sub-pixel grid
  alignment. Refusals by station range from 40 (ITCSM_15) to 71 (ITCSM_08). No station
  is spared, because every station sees both S1 zones (relative orbits 88 and 15 put
  their frames in different zones).
- **Point mode accepts all 3,505.** Every layer was read at the declared station point,
  inside the layer, with the sampled pixel within half its diagonal of the point. The
  decision "point sampling … no common grid, no resampling" is recorded on every sample.
  The distance between the S1 (10 m) and S2 (10/20 m) sampled pixel centres: **median
  7.06 m, 95th percentile 11.52 m, max 14.11 m**, all within the ≤ 21 m that a 10 m
  and a 20 m pixel containing the same point allow.
- **What each choice would mean.**
  - **Grid mode as is** removes 27.9% of samples, non-randomly: they are the
    samples whose S1 pass came from the other UTM zone.
  - **Keeping them under grid mode** requires reprojecting one sensor onto the
    other's grid, a resampling step the cube does not perform today (not
    implemented).
  - **Point mode** keeps every sample with no resampling, at the cost of declaring
    that this cube is point-sampled, not an image stack. That is a change to the
    method, which the provenance log would record on every sample.
- **The local two-station run showed grid-mode verdicts can depend on S2 tile choice:**
  a different tile for the same acquisition swaps which passes are refused. On the
  cluster, tiles were chosen by station-pixel coverage on 1 km chips, so these counts
  reflect that rule.

### Resolution

All 3,505 samples carry the decision that, with no target GSD supplied, the cube
defaults to the coarsest input (20 m) and records each 10 m layer as downsampled
(21,030 layer decisions). In this point-sampled cube no resampling actually happens: each
value is read at its native pixel. The decision describes a grid cube.
**The resolution gate assumes a stack, just as grid-mode geometry does**, and that
should be decided together with the geometry question.

### Temporal alignment

**1,415 samples (40.4%)** have their S2 observation more than 7 days before the S1
pass: PASS_WITH_DECISION, "retained and flagged rather than dropped". **No sample uses a
future S2 observation:** the assembly forbids it, and the gate confirms it. This is the
gate's working population for the ablation.

### Quality and missingness: 110 refusals, none of them fill

The S1 range fix moved only this gate, and only its decisions (next section).
**`inspect_refusals.py` (job 247675) re-read every refused sample's chips.** The 110
refusals come from 24 S2 observations, concentrated on winter dates (2019-11-24: 30;
02-15: 24; 04-16: 15):

| Trigger | Samples | Offending pixels, by SCL |
|---|---|---|
| B02 below 50 DN on > 1% of the chip | **102** | vegetation 60%, dark area 32%, cloud shadow 4%, unclassified 4% |
| B02/B03/B04/B08 above 12,000 on > 1% of the chip | 5 (overlapping) | high-probability cloud 100% |
| a chip > 30% nodata | 6 | S1 frame edges (no S2 layer exceeded 30%) |

**None of the 110 is fill masquerading as data**, the failure the plausibility check
was built for (B02 = 1 at a swath edge). In every case the station pixel itself passed
the per-pixel rules. The refusals are about **other pixels in the 1 km chip**:
- dark winter vegetation under low sun, which genuinely reads below 50 DN after
  atmospheric correction;
- bright cloud, which genuinely exceeds 12,000;
- S1 frame edges.

The 392 decisions are of the same kind:
- 355 + 88 range excursions within the 1% tolerance;
- 170 single-band zero-fill notes;
- 76 retained-invalid notes.

**Two more declared bounds are guesses that do not hold** (open, not changed in this
run):
1. **The B02 ≥ 50 floor.** It was set from the one fill value observed (B02 = 1). Real
   dark winter vegetation falls below it. It is used in three places, so it also
   affects **which S2 dates count as usable at the station**, and therefore the samples
   themselves:
   - fetch-time tile scoring;
   - the per-pixel usability rule;
   - the gate.
2. **The 12,000 ceiling on the other S2 bands.** Bright cloud exceeds it. The ceiling
   adds nothing a fill check needs; fill is small.

**A layer-level gate on a point-sampled cube judges ground the sample never uses.**
The refusals are correct statements about the chips, and irrelevant to the sample pixel.
That is the layer-versus-pixel finding from the swath-edge work, now seen at scale.

PASS on all 3,505:

- **S2** is declared L2A surface reflectance.
- **No band arithmetic is declared**, so no expression was checked. The gate's dB and

## The S1 plausible range was wrong

**The first run (job 247671) declared S1 γ⁰ plausible in [10⁻⁵, 10] (linear).** The
upper bound was a guess. Real VV reached **1043** (+30 dB) and VH 182: buildings and
corner reflectors, real and very bright.

**What it cost.** **1,128 S1 layer decisions** in the first run, across **977 samples
(27.9%)** whose only quality finding was this bound. The quality verdict moved from
PASS_WITH_DECISION to PASS for exactly those samples:

| quality_missingness | Job 247671, [10⁻⁵, 10] | Job 247673, [10⁻⁵, ∞) |
|---|---|---|
| PASS | 2,026 | **3,003** |
| PASS_WITH_DECISION | 1,369 | **392** |
| REFUSE | 110 | **110** |

**Nothing else moved:** samples, drops, the east/west ages, and every other gate's counts
are identical between the two runs. That checks that the fix did only what it should.

**How it was found.**
1. **The local two-station run first showed it:** VV up to 1043 in the chips, and
   0.003–0.07% of pixels flagged.
2. **The full run showed its scale.** Within the 1% tolerance it produced decisions, not
   refusals; a slightly stricter tolerance would have turned 977 decisions into
   refusals of real data.

**The fix declares only a lower bound, (10⁻⁵, None).** The failure the check exists for
is fill masquerading as data, and fill is small (`tests/test_io.py`:
`test_strong_reflector_does_not_trigger_plausibility`,
`test_sub_threshold_fill_triggers_plausibility`).

**For the paper: declared ranges are guesses, and a gate is only as good as its
declarations.** Three of the three plausibility bounds declared for this cube were wrong
in the same direction:
- the S1 ceiling;
- the S2 ceiling;
- the B02 floor.

Each would refuse or flag real measurements. The gate behaved correctly every time; the
declarations did not. Running the gates on real data is what exposed them, which is an
argument for running contracts on real data before trusting their verdicts.

## East/west S2 age, with cloud applied

**The ratio narrowed, the absolute gap widened.**

| | Availability only (prior; PLAN.md) | Cloud-aware (this cube) |
|---|---|---|
| East (ITCSM_01, 04, 07, 09) mean S2 age | 2.66 d | **9.53 d** (median 7.80) |
| Other 13 | 1.41 d | **6.78 d** (median 4.80) |
| Ratio | 1.88 | **1.41** |
| Absolute gap | 1.25 d | **2.75 d** |

The ratio fell from 1.88 to 1.41 once clouds applied: **the relative difference is
smaller than the availability-only figures suggested.** The absolute gap more than
doubled, because clouds lengthen every station's wait, and the eastern stations, with
half the dates, wait longest.

The stratified block decision is re-checked on these figures in PLAN.md. **It holds.**
Stratified folds still have the smallest cross-fold age spread (0.53–1.08 d, against
1.23–3.19 d for plain spatial blocks and 1.30 d for dropping the four).

## Decisions waiting on these numbers

1. **Geometry: grid mode or point mode.** Grid mode refuses 979 samples (27.9%); point
   mode accepts all, with the method change recorded. If point mode: the resolution
   gate's stack assumption needs the same treatment.
2. **The B02 ≥ 50 floor and the S2 12,000 ceiling.** Both refuse or flag real values,
   and the floor also shapes sample selection.
3. **Layer- versus pixel-level quality** for a point-sampled cube: whether the quality
   gate should judge the chip, the station pixel, or both.
